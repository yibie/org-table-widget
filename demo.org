* Quick controls

- =M-x org-table-widget-mode=: enable or disable rendering in this buffer.
- =M-x org-table-widget-toggle=: reveal or render the table at point.
- =M-x org-table-widget-refresh=: redraw every table in the buffer.
- Enter a rendered table to edit its original Org source; leave it to render
  the widget again.
